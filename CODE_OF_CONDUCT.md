# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be straightforward and be kind. Critique code, not people. Assume the person
you are replying to is competent and short of context, because that is almost
always what is true.

## Expected

- Technical disagreement, stated plainly, with reasons.
- Reviews that say what is wrong and what would be right.
- Patience with people who are new to Go, to this framework, or to the
  problem it addresses.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, demeaning comments, or harassment of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
somebody is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers at **conduct@inceptools.dev**. Reports are
read only by the maintainers and are not shared further without the reporter's
agreement.

Maintainers will respond with a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban,
proportionate to what happened and to whether it is a pattern. A maintainer who
breaks these rules is held to them more strictly, not less.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
